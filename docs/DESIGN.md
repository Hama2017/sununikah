## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined synthesis of sacred tradition, contemporary matrimonial intent, and understated luxury. Built around the ethos of *halal* union, intentionality, and moral serenity, the aesthetic departs firmly from the hyperactive, gamified patterns of modern dating apps. It replaces casual swiping with dignified contemplation.

The design philosophy is **Minimalisme Chaleureux & Spirituel** (Warm Spiritual Minimalism):
- **Sanctuary-like Clarity:** Abundant breathing room, calm rhythmic layouts, and an absence of sensory clutter ensure interactions feel thoughtful, safe, and deliberate.
- **Architectural Reverence:** Evoking the calm symmetry and timeless grandeur of sacred architecture through proportional harmony, subtle golden hairline dividing borders, and editorial poise.
- **Dignified Trust:** Tactile surfaces, muted champagne highlights, and velvety emerald surfaces create a private salon ambiance designed to reassure both candidates and their families.

## Colors

The palette balances the regal gravitas of ancient sanctuaries with the warm hospitality of natural parchment and brushed precious metals:

- **Primary (`#123C30` - Deep Majestic Emerald):** Represents enduring commitment, grounding, and moral stability. Used for dominant brand moments, high-level headers, primary buttons, and deeply focused surface backdrops. Supported by `#0C2B22` for high-contrast typography and deep navigation frames, with `#1A4F40` for structural containers.
- **Secondary (`#C7A76D` - Brushed Champagne Gold):** Serves as an accent of refinement and sacred value. Employed for verification seals, active states, key milestone badges, and decorative hairline borders. Softened by `#DFC594` for warm subtle glows.
- **Tertiary (`#387D65` - Soft Mint Leaf):** A comforting, restorative accent used for success states, active communication cues, chaperon/wali endorsements, and positive value-alignment indicators.
- **Neutral & Canvas (`#FDFBF7` / `#F5F1E8` - Luminous Warm Ivory):** The primary experiential canvas. Prevents the clinical detachment of pure digital white (`#FFFFFF`) while generating a radiant, paper-like warmth. `#E2D9CC` provides gentle structural delineation without stark visual breaks.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical editorial poise with structural legibility:

- **Playfair Display** introduces warmth, poetic restraint, and timeless nobility. Reserved for introspective prompts, names, life mission summaries, and sectional milestones.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides clarity and modern balance. Its geometric structure ensures high legibility on mobile screens for biodata, spiritual habits, personal preferences, and communicative exchanges.
- **Micro-Copy Conventions:** Badges, trust tags, and categorizations always adopt `label-sm` or `label-md` in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mirror luxury house cataloguing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces calm spatial composition over density. Mobile layouts strictly observe safe viewing boundaries while providing visual grounding.

- **Mobile Rhythm:** A 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` screen margins and a `1rem` inter-column gutter. Vertical rhythm relies on ample separation (`space-xl` between logical content blocks) to foster contemplative review of profiles rather than impulsive interaction.
- **Tablet / Responsive Slate:** Expands to an 8-column layout with max-width bounding boxes of `640px` for reading and profile inspection views, preserving intimate, portrait-oriented visual balances.
- **Negative Space Directive:** Content groups (e.g., "Projet de Vie", "Pratique Spirituelle") must be insulated with `space-lg` internal container padding and never compressed against actionable interface controls.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain the organic, luxurious character of warm minimalism, elevation rejects artificial dropshadows in favor of tonal layering and brushed gold micro-borders:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Luminous Warm Ivory (`#FDFBF7`) without shadows.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Containers):** Tinted Soft Sand (`#F5F1E8`) wrapped with a 1px micro-border in `#E2D9CC` or brushed champagne gold `#C7A76D` at 30% opacity.
- **Level 2 (Modals & Focus Trays):** Pure off-white containers seated atop an ambient diffuse shadow tinted with primary deep emerald: `0px 12px 36px -8px rgba(12, 43, 34, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Direct Navigation & Affirmative Action):** Grounded emerald pills (`#123C30`) elevated with an emerald-hued ambient cast: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(18, 60, 48, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The form language uses balanced rounded forms that feel smooth and protective without looking child-like or overly casual:

- **Cards & Content Frames:** Structured at `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) for an inviting architectural curvature.
- **Interactive Badges & Trust Seals:** Structured at `rounded-md` (`0.5rem` / 8px) or fully oval pills to represent verified status.
- **Primary CTAs:** Rounded capsules and smooth rectilinear forms (`0.75rem` / 12px to `1rem` / 16px) that provide substantial, reassuring touch targets.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary CTA ("Poursuivre avec Intention", "Exprimer un Intérêt"):** Background in Deep Emerald (`#123C30`), text in Warm Ivory (`#FDFBF7`), subtle hairline border in Brushed Gold (`#C7A76D` at 40%). Height: 52px minimum for deliberate physical engagement.
- **Secondary CTA:** Background in Ivory (`#FDFBF7`), text in Emerald (`#123C30`), border in 1px solid `#E2D9CC`. On press, transitions to `#F5F1E8`.
- **Tertiary / Chaperon Action:** Subtle ghost button with text styled in `#1A4F40`, accompanied by an icon in Champagne Gold (`#C7A76D`).

### Verified Badges ("Intention Vérifiée" & "Profil Validé")
- Encapsulated badge containing an 8-point geometric rosette or clean check icon.
- Background: Soft Ivory Tint (`#FDFBF7`) with a 1px border in Champagne Gold (`#C7A76D`).
- Typography: `label-sm`, uppercase, colored in `#0C2B22`.

### Profile Cards & Content Containers
- Focus is placed on life project narratives, moral values, and family outlook rather than oversized photo galleries alone.
- Surfaces use `#F5F1E8` with a 1px outer frame in `#E2D9CC`.
- Quotes and spiritual values are styled using `headline-sm` in *Playfair Display* with a slight italic treatment.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: Pure Warm Ivory (`#FDFBF7`).
- Border: 1px solid `#E2D9CC`; shifts to `#C7A76D` on focus with no glowing ring.
- Labels: `label-md` set above inputs in deep emerald (`#123C30`) for authoritative clarity.

### Selection Chips & Value Tags
- Used for attributes like prayer cadence, family vision, hobbies, and relocation openness.
- Unselected: Background `#F5F1E8`, text `#1A4F40`, border in `#E2D9CC`.
- Selected: Background `#123C30`, text `#FDFBF7`, border in `#C7A76D`.
